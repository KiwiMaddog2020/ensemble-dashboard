Link your own Anthropic key to use Claude on your own terms. Free users get a starting platform allotment of 5 calls/day; linking your key removes that limit. Pro users get a 500/month platform allotment plus unlimited usage with their own key.

You're bound by Anthropic's Acceptable Use Policy when using your own key. Don't use it for anything that violates Anthropic's terms.
