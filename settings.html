Stored locally in ~/.claude/orchestrator/.env.local at chmod 600. Used for Studio generation in preference to the platform default. Get a key at console.anthropic.com — $5 covers ~250 generations.

Anthropic API key

Link your own Anthropic key to use Claude on your own terms. Free users get a starting platform allotment of 5 calls/day; linking your key removes that limit. Pro users get a 500/month platform allotment plus unlimited usage with their own key.

Checking BYOK status…

Heads up: When you link your Anthropic key, your Claude conversations go directly from your key to Anthropic's API. Endenza doesn't store or proxy your prompts.

You're bound by Anthropic's Acceptable Use Policy when using your own key. Don't use it for anything that violates Anthropic's terms.

You can revoke or rotate your key from this page anytime.
