Live session mirror

Operator power-tool

The Terminal mirrors your Endenza from your Mac.

This is the operator cockpit — a live Terminal for the Claude session running on your own Mac. Pair a runner and this pane streams your transcript and agent activity straight to the browser. With DRIVE enabled, it also sends input and permission decisions back to that runner-owned session. It’s the private-controls side of Endenza, so it stays dark until a runner is paired. You don’t need it to build.

  1. Build & ship in your Studio — or generate a world from any GitHub repo.
  2. Plan & coordinate with Maestro, your orchestration room.
  3. Track everything from your Dashboard.

Run Endenza from your Mac? The Terminal is yours. to get your one-line install command, paste it into your Mac’s Terminal, and the banner above flips from connecting to live on its own. That single command installs the runner, pairs it, and starts it on login. Already running a runner? Pair this browser.
