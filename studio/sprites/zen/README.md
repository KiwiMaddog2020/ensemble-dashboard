## Aesthetic

The Zen pack evokes the pleasure of analogue writing: crisp paper, the weight of a fountain pen, the smell of ink. Sprites use a soft, layered approach—delicate opacity stacks rather than solid shapes—to create depth without noise. The result is quiet, considered, and tactile.
