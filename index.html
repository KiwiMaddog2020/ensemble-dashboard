A CITY

The Ensemble
is everyone.

A navigable multiplayer pixel world of creator showcases. Walk into anyone’s canvas as a sprite. Leave notes pinned to others' work. Browse the feed. Buy and sell sprites, themes, and setups in the marketplace. A list of links is alone. Ensemble is a place where every page is alongside every other.
