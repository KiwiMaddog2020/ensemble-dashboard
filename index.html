THREE PILLARS

Build. Share. Inspire.

Link your GitHub and your studio builds itself, no install. Open a door and your work joins a world raised by everyone. Then publish your setup so the next person can work the way you do, with the agents running underneath.

  1. 01

    CREATE

    Link your GitHub and each repo becomes a walkable 16-bit room you can step inside: a tiny browser game, a track, a long-form piece, all playable. Your studio builds itself. Or start fresh in the Studio.

  2. 02

    SHARE YOUR WORK

    Your studio joins the Ensemble, a walkable world of everyone's creations. People walk up to your arcade cabinet and play your game, hit your vinyl, read your essay. The room is the share.

  3. 03

    SHARE YOUR SETUP

    Publish your Suite to the Library so others can work the way you do: CLAUDE.md, skills, themes, the Maestro and Council mix, autopilot defaults. The runner and agents are the engine that powers it. One install and they are set up like you.

↺ Every setup shared sets up the next person.
