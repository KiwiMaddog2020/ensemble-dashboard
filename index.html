A CITY

Endenza
is everyone.

A navigable multiplayer pixel world of creator showcases. Walk into anyone’s canvas as a sprite. Leave notes pinned to others' work. Browse the feed. Buy and sell sprites, themes, and setups in the marketplace. A list of links is alone. Endenza is a place where every page is alongside every other.

Multiplayer city: v1.5 (months 13–15)

Open the chat drawer and Claude becomes your collaborator. Describe a sprite, an interaction, or a whole canvas — Claude builds it into your Studio. Apply, tweak, or discard. Bring your own key, or use the free tier. Direct creation with the smartest model around.
